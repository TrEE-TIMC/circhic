First, one can adjust the data plotted by setting the maximum inner and outer genomic distance of the contact count being plotted. The inner distance corresponds to the &quot;lower triangular&quot; matrix of the contact count maps, an the outter distance to the upper triangular matrix. The data is thus symmetric to the midpoint, ie the &quot;diagonal&quot; of the contact count matrix.">
